# Continuity Ledger

> Purpose: the four lists that keep a long work from contradicting itself — what is the case, who knows it, what is owed, what things are called.
> Use when: writing chapter two onwards, continuing someone else's material, or before any session where the work is longer than you can hold.
> Pairs with: `references/continuity-memory.md` — the admission rule, the sweep, and when to stop tracking. `templates/timeline.md` — dates and elapsed time.

How to use: add rows in the chapter being written, not after the draft. Sort by entity rather than by chapter — contradictions sit next to each other in entity order and are invisible in narrative order. Update `reader knew` only when the reader actually learns something; it is the column that catches dramatic irony being spent by accident.

When to stop: when a row cannot contradict anything, delete it. The ledger is not a wiki and its value is inversely proportional to its length — the version that gets read before every chapter beats the version that is complete.
