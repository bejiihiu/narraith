# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Giving and accepting constructive feedback on craft, with evidence from the text.
- Respecting that literary judgement is plural: a disagreement about whether a scene works is not a
  personal attack.
- Being precise about what a source does and does not show.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, including about a person's writing.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Using the issue tracker to argue that a group of people writes badly.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, and contributions that violate this code, and
will communicate reasons when they do. Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer listed in
`CITATION.cff`. Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
